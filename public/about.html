About PBAG

Nurturing talent. Creating art. Transforming communities.

PBAG (Peers Best Art Group) is a community-based arts organisation founded on 23rd August 2011 by like-minded young artists from Limuru, Kenya, under the leadership of Boni Kanyuira — the brainchild behind its formation.

Act I · Who we are

From a youth group to a consortium

PBAG was established to bring young people together around a common purpose: identifying, nurturing, developing and showcasing artistic talent, while using the arts to address real issues affecting society. From its inception, PBAG embraced Theatre for Community Development as a tool for entertainment, education, community engagement, awareness creation and social transformation.

Over the years PBAG has grown into a broader artistic consortium bringing different creative entities together under one vision. Our work spans theatre and other creative disciplines — giving artists room to develop their skills, collaborate, create meaningful work and contribute to the cultural and social development of their communities.

  1. 2011Founded on 23 August in Limuru by young artists led by Boni Kanyuira.
  2. Theatre for DevelopmentDrama becomes PBAG’s tool for awareness, education and community dialogue.
  3. The ConsortiumPBAG Theatre, Peatice Productions, Peers Got Talanta and PBAG Bunge unite under one vision.
  4. 41 membersMost drawn from Limuru Sub-County, Kiambu County — and growing.
  5. TodayLive shows, a 7th-edition talent festival, digital ticketing, a virtual theatre and a Generative AI studio.

Our purpose

A platform where talent is identified, nurtured, developed and transformed into meaningful artistic expression — empowering young people and using art as a vehicle for positive change.

Our mission

To identify, nurture and execute talents and skills for excellence and God’s glory.

Our vision

To be a world-class group that observes disciplined execution of its artwork.

Act II · Core values

Four values, one ensemble

U

Unity

We believe in the strength of working together. Collaboration, teamwork and shared purpose are central to our artistic and organisational growth.

L

Love

Respect, care, inclusion and genuine support for one another, our audiences, our communities and the wider creative fraternity.

S

Sacrifice

Meaningful artistic work requires commitment, dedication and a willingness to give of ourselves for a greater purpose.

D

Discipline

Professionalism, responsibility, commitment and excellence in the execution of our artistic work.

Act III · Management

The team

PBAG Consortium is guided by a five-member management team, linked to PBAG Bunge — our lead subsidiary for leadership and governance.

Effective management is built on teamwork, accountability, discipline, creativity and shared responsibility — everyone contributing their expertise towards one vision.
