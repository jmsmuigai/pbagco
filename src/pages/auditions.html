Is it free to audition?

Yes. PBAG never charges audition fees. Be wary of anyone asking you to pay to audition in PBAG's name — report it on 0720 960 180.

Can under-18s apply?

Yes, with a parent's or guardian's consent; a guardian must accompany you on audition day.

What should my clip show?

One to three minutes of your best work — a monologue, a set, a dance, a song or a reel. Phone video is perfectly fine.

How will I hear back?

By WhatsApp/SMS or email. You can also track your status here with your reference number at any time.
