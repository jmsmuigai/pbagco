Our subsidiaries

One consortium. Four specialised arms.

Together they let PBAG work across Theatre, Entertainment & Media, Community Development, and Leadership & Civic Engagement — a connected ecosystem where creativity becomes a pathway to talent, opportunity and social impact.

PBAG Theatre Peatice Productions Peers Got Talanta (PGT) CBO PBAG Bunge
Arm 01
Professional Theatre & Theatre for Development

PBAG Theatre

PBAG Theatre produces professional theatre productions, issue-based performances and Theatre for Development programmes that use drama and performance to engage audiences and communities around relevant social and cultural issues.

To create impactful theatre that entertains, educates, provokes dialogue and contributes to social and cultural development.

Mission

  • Stage productions & seasons
  • Issue-based & forum theatre
  • Theatre for Development programmes
  • Schools & community tours

Arm 02
Entertainment, Media & Content

Peatice Productions

Peatice Productions develops and produces comedy stage and screen shows, short-form content, entertainment media and creative resources — providing platforms for artists while creating engaging content for contemporary audiences.

To create entertaining, innovative and accessible content that provides opportunities for creative talent and connects audiences with compelling stories and experiences.

Mission

  • Comedy stage & screen shows
  • Short-form content creation
  • Entertainment media production
  • Creative resources for artists

Arm 03
Community, Talent & Social Impact

Peers Got Talanta (PGT) CBO

Peers Got Talanta (PGT) CBO works at community level through outreach, talent discovery, youth empowerment, NAC Week, creative entrepreneurship and social-impact programmes. Its flagship PGT Festival is now in its 7th edition, with categories from Tech & Digital Innovation to Comedy, Theatre & Film.

To discover and nurture talent, empower young people and use creativity as a catalyst for community development, entrepreneurship and positive social change.

Mission

  • PGT Festival talent search
  • Youth empowerment & mentorship
  • NAC Week (Arts & Culture Week)
  • Creative entrepreneurship

Arm 04
Leadership, Civic Engagement & AdvocacyLead subsidiary

PBAG Bunge

PBAG Bunge — bunge is Kiswahili for parliament — provides platforms for leadership development, civic engagement, policy dialogue, governance forums, mentorship and youth advocacy, encouraging informed participation in community and public affairs. As the lead subsidiary it is home to the Consortium’s management team.

To cultivate informed, responsible and engaged citizens by creating spaces for leadership, dialogue, mentorship and civic participation.

Mission

  • Leadership development
  • Policy dialogue & governance forums
  • Mentorship
  • Youth advocacy
