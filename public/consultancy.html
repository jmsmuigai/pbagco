Services

What we do

AI for creative production

Use generative AI to brainstorm scripts, storyboard scenes, draft posters and caption content — with humans firmly in the director’s chair.

Smart bots & AI ushers

Gemini-powered assistants for your website, WhatsApp or box office that answer questions, sell tickets and take bookings 24/7 — like Cue on this site.

Audience & campaign analytics

Turn ticket sales, social engagement and survey data into dashboards that show what works and who your audience is.

AI literacy for artists & youth

Hands-on workshops that teach young creatives to use AI tools productively, safely and ethically — and to protect their own work.

Responsible AI, IP & data

Guidance on copyright, consent, likeness rights and Kenya’s Data Protection Act when using AI with creative work and audience data.

AI-powered Theatre for Development

Pair forum theatre with AI — multilingual scripts, rapid community surveys and impact reporting — for NGOs and county programmes.

How we work

Four acts

Every engagement follows the same arc, scaled to your budget — from a single workshop to a full AI-enabled platform.

  1. DiscoverA free call to understand your goals, audience and data.
  2. DesignWe map where AI genuinely helps — and where it shouldn’t.
  3. BuildPrototypes on Google Gemini & Cloud, tested with real users.
  4. Train & hand overYour team learns to run, measure and improve it.

For AI agents: PBAG speaks MCP

This site exposes a Model Context Protocol endpoint at pbagco.com/mcp, so assistants like Claude or Gemini can look up PBAG shows, check seat availability and file booking requests on a user’s behalf. Ask us to make your organisation agent-ready too.
