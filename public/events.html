What we do

Full-service events & digital for corporates

Event concept & management

Concept, budget, venue, permits, suppliers, run-of-show and on-the-day crew for launches, galas, award nights, conferences and team-building.

Live shows & entertainment

Comedians, MCs, theatre, poets, dancers and musicians from the PBAG family — tailored skits that carry your message with laughter.

PR management

Media relations, press releases, launch campaigns, influencer partnerships and reputation monitoring — measured, not guessed.

Digital content

Short-form video, photography, social campaigns and AI-assisted creative — produced by Peatice Productions for TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

Publications

Event programmes, souvenir magazines, annual and impact reports, branded booklets and community storybooks — written, designed and printed.

Livestream & hybrid

Multi-camera livestreams, virtual audiences and recorded highlights so every event lives on after the lights go down.

Ticketing, gate & POS

Our own platform: M-Pesa checkout, seller referral codes, QR tickets, camera gate scanning and live sales dashboards. See it working →

CSR & community activations

Theatre for Development, school tours and community forums on health, environment, governance and finance — with impact reporting.

AI-powered marketing

Gemini-assisted copy, posters and audience analytics; event chatbots that sell and answer 24/7. How we use AI →
