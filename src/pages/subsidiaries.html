Community, Talent & Social Impact

Peers Got Talanta (PGT) CBO

Peers Got Talanta (PGT) CBO works at community level through outreach, talent discovery, youth empowerment, NAC Week, creative entrepreneurship and social-impact programmes. Its flagship PGT Festival is now in its 7th edition, with categories from Tech & Digital Innovation to Comedy, Theatre & Film.

To discover and nurture talent, empower young people and use creativity as a catalyst for community development, entrepreneurship and positive social change.

PBAG Bunge

PBAG Bunge is the lead subsidiary and central governing organ of the PBAG Consortium. Its mandate extends across all subsidiaries — PBAG Bunge, PBAG Community Theatre, Peatice Productions and Peers Got Talanta (PGT) CBO — providing strategic leadership, institutional oversight, coordination and accountability so that the Consortium fulfils its mission, vision and objectives.

To lead, oversee and coordinate the PBAG Consortium with transparency and accountability — so that every subsidiary delivers on the shared mission, vision and objectives.
