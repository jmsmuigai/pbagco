The toolchain

One ecosystem, every stage of production

01 · Research

NotebookLM

Upload archives, interviews and books; ask questions grounded in your own sources — how we organised the research behind Ithaka cia Kamĩrĩĩthũ.

02 · Writing

Gemini

Brainstorm premises, beat sheets and dialogue in English, Kiswahili and Gĩkũyũ; translate and adapt for schools, radio or screen.

03 · Prototyping

Google AI Studio

Test prompts, compare models and turn them into small apps — script doctors, casting assistants, audience chatbots like Cue.

04 · Visual development

Nano Banana

Gemini's image model for concept art, costume and set boards, consistent characters across frames, and poster key art.

05 · Previz & teasers

Veo

Generate short video shots from text or images — animatics, mood reels and social teasers before the camera rolls.

06 · AI filmmaking

Flow

Google's filmmaking workspace that brings Veo and image tools together to build, extend and sequence scenes.

07 · Build

Antigravity

An agentic development platform for building the digital side of a production — websites, ticketing, dashboards and AI agents.

08 · Launch

Google Cloud

Host the box office and AI usher on Cloud Run with Firestore — fast in Kenya, scales for opening night.
